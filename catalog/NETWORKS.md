# Given or generated: how each chemistry gets its network

Every catalog entry records `network: given` or `network: generated` (see `catalog/SCHEMA.md`). This table is the reasoning behind each value.

- **given**: the chemistry *is* a reaction network. Chemart instantiates it straight from the parameters: written down, built by a formula, or drawn once at random. Running it means dynamics on that network.
- **generated**: the network is the *output* of the chemistry's algorithm: rules applied to the molecules' own structure until closure, or the record of what a simulation did. It cannot be known without running the procedure.

*Borderline* marks entries where the other reading is defensible; the note says why the given reading was chosen or what would change it.
